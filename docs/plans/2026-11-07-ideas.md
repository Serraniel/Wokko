# Ideas for Wokko

Nothing here is built or promised. Each idea needs the owner's yes. Ordered by how much it would help for the effort, and all of them keep to the rule that Wokko never calls an AI itself.

## Best value

| Idea | Why it's good | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| **"What can I cook?"** Type or tick what's in the kitchen, and get recipes sorted by how little is missing, with the missing items one tap from the shopping list | Turns the recipe collection into an answer to the daily question. The data is already there | Small |
| **Reminders on the plan** "Soak the beans tonight" from a recipe's long waiting times, shown on the day before | Catches the mistake planning is meant to prevent | Small |
| **Leftovers** Cook 8 servings, eat 4, and have the plan offer the rest for tomorrow | Uses the multi-day plans and cook log that exist already | Small |
| **Install as an app, with offline recipes** A proper home-screen app that keeps recently opened recipes and the shopping list available in a shop with no signal | Shops often have poor reception. Needs care: stale app code is a stability risk, which is why the overhaul left this out | Medium |
| **Share to Wokko** Share a page from the phone's browser straight into the import | The fastest way to collect recipes. Needs HTTPS and the installed app | Small, after the above |
| **Bring in other collections** Import from Tandoor exports, Mealie and Paprika files, and the schema.org JSON many apps write | Makes moving to Wokko painless for others too, and the importer is most of the work | Medium |

## Cooking

- **Hands-free steps:** "next" and "back" by voice in live cooking, processed in the browser where it's supported.
- **Cook together:** a live-cooking link where everyone sees the same step and timers.
- **Ingredients at the moment they're used:** highlight the amounts inside a step's text, scaled to the servings.
- **Notes per recipe:** "use less salt next time", shown at the top the next time you cook it. The cook log already collects these comments.

## Planning and shopping

- **Pantry staples:** mark salt or oil as "always at home", and skip them when a recipe goes on the list.
- **Plan from favourites:** fill a week with well-rated recipes not cooked lately, then let the person swap any of them.
- **Week templates:** save and reuse a good week.
- **Calendar feed:** a private iCal link, so the meal plan shows in any calendar.
- **Plan and cost:** optional prices per ingredient, for an estimate per week.

## Collection

- **Recipe variants:** "my version" linked to the original, keeping both.
- **Scan a recipe from a photo:** text recognition could run in the browser, with no AI service. Quality on handwriting would need testing first.
- **Duplicates:** warn when an import looks like a recipe already saved. kitshn's import screen has room for this too.
- **Weight or volume per food:** convert "1 cup flour" to grams, and count more ingredients in nutrition.
- **Printing:** a cookbook PDF made from a recipe book, as a gift.

## Running it

- **Off-site backups:** an optional encrypted copy to any S3-compatible storage. Only the owner's NAS protects against a disk failure today, and only once it's set up.
- **Backup restore in the admin page:** today it needs the command line. This must stay very careful.
- **More MCP tools:** `plan_week` prompts, and resources so an assistant can browse recipes without a search.
